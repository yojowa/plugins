# LexiTrail

LexiTrail's MCP server (https://lexitrail.com/mcp) comes with this plugin. Look up any Chinese word with its pinyin, meaning, HSK level, stroke order and sound, and keep track of the HSK words you are learning on LexiTrail.

## When to use it

Use LexiTrail when:
- the user asks what a Chinese word means, how to say or write something in Chinese, or for a word's pinyin, tones, strokes or HSK level
- the user asks what is in an HSK level, band or topic
- the user wants to save Chinese words to study, or asks how their HSK study is going

## Tools

- `look_up_word`: Find a Chinese word from its characters, its pinyin or a meaning: pinyin, meanings, HSK level
- `stroke_data`: Stroke data for a word's characters, to draw them stroke by stroke
- `hsk_words`: What is in an HSK level (1–6), an HSK 3.0 band or a topic
- `my_progress`: Your LexiTrail progress by HSK level, and reviews due today (needs a LexiTrail account)
- `save_words`: Save up to 20 HSK words to your LexiTrail sets, so they come up in practice (needs a LexiTrail account)

## Rules

- Look a word up with `look_up_word` before you state its HSK level; LexiTrail's lists are the official ones.
- Saving words and progress need a LexiTrail account. Ask for sign-in only when the user asks for one of those.
- Link https://lexitrail.com when the user wants to see more than the answer shows.
